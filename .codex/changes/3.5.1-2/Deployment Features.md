# MovieApp 3.5.1 Store Features

The text below is written for customers discovering MovieApp in the stores. It describes what customers can do without referring to databases, background jobs, code changes, previous behavior, or internal deployment work.

## Apple App Store

Apple customers are currently on MovieApp 3.4.2, so the App Store description includes the complete customer-facing feature set they will receive in 3.5.1.

```text
* Find movies in the languages you want to watch.
  * Choose one or more original languages in Advanced Search.
  * Start with English selected, or change it to match your preferences.
  * Browse languages alphabetically and swipe sideways to see more choices.
  * Keep your language choices while refreshing results or browsing additional pages.

* Know when a movie may require renting or buying.
  * Look for the yellow shopping bag on movie posters.
  * The bag appears when a movie is not available through a subscription or an ad-supported stream.
  * Movies available through a subscription or with ads do not display the bag.
  * See this guidance in Favorites, Movies I Have Seen, Search by Movie Title, and Advanced Search.

* Refresh movie collections and searches with a simple pull.
  * Pull down on Home to load the latest movie collections.
  * Refresh a title search without entering the movie title again.
  * Refresh an Advanced Search without rebuilding your filters.
  * Continue browsing additional pages from the newly refreshed results.

* Enjoy dependable movie poster displays throughout the app.
  * Posters that do not load immediately are automatically tried again.
  * A familiar placeholder appears instead of an empty poster area when an image is unavailable.
  * The same reliable poster experience is available across Home, searches, filmographies, and Movie Details.

* Bug Fix - Optimized Movie Detail page to render faster.
```
