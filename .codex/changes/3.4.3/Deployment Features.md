# MovieApp 3.4.3 Store Features

The text below is written for customers discovering MovieApp in the store. It describes what customers can do without referring to implementation details or internal development work.

```text
* Keep browsing when a movie poster is temporarily unavailable.
  * MovieApp automatically tries again when a poster does not load the first time.
  * A familiar placeholder appears instead of an empty poster area when an image is unavailable.
  * The same dependable poster experience is available across Home, searches, filmographies, and Movie Details.

* Refresh movie collections and searches with a simple pull.
  * Pull down on Home to load the latest movie collections.
  * Refresh a title search without entering the movie title again.
  * Refresh an Advanced Search without rebuilding your filters.
  * Continue browsing additional pages from the newly refreshed results.

* See complete Home movie collections when the app opens.
  * Home prepares its movie sections together while keeping every existing category and poster card.
  * Available movie sections remain ready to browse even if another section cannot be loaded.
  * Posters that have already loaded remain quick to display during later visits.
```
